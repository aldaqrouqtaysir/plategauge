PlateGauge is an AI-assisted project, not a claim of unaided solo
implementation.

- Authorized implementation of the locked PlateGauge plan.

experiment execution, testing, analysis, documentation, interface, and

[public AI-assistance disclosure](AI_ASSISTANCE_PUBLIC.md).

## Evidence not established by the repository

Independent frozen-path reproduction, final contribution-language review, and
release authorization were not complete at source freeze. Until separately
recorded, do not claim independent end-to-end implementation, complete
technical review, deployment, user testing, or operational impact.

Any later public contribution statement must match the dated approval and
review evidence associated with the exact released commit.
